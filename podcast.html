What I Took From It

Taking theory into a real classroom

Working on Episode 4 of Learning How to Learn with my classmates was one of the most interesting parts of the course, because we had to take what we read about Language Learning Strategies (LLS) and Self-Regulated Learning (SRL) and figure out how it actually works inside a classroom. Instead of repeating definitions, our conversation focused on how teachers can explicitly teach these strategies and help students plan, monitor, and evaluate their own learning without solving everything for them.

Preparing and recording the episode made me look at the whole topic from a teacher’s perspective. Talking about the gap between academic research and what really happens in schools reminded me that generic study advice rarely fits every student. As a future teacher, I need to pay close attention to my students’ context, show them concrete strategies in action, and give them space to figure out what works best for them.
