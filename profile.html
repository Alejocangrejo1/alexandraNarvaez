What It Shows

Learning on the fly, through context and people

Looking at my SILL profile, with an overall average of 3.4, I realized that I learn English mostly by interacting with other people and guessing things out as I go. Part F (Learning with others) was my highest score at 4.0, and honestly that makes a lot of sense because I always try to ask classmates for help, practice with them, and I am not afraid to ask questions when I do not understand something. I also scored 3.7 in compensation strategies, since I constantly guess word meanings from context when reading.

What is less developed in my profile is Part A (Remembering more effectively), which only reached 2.9. This surprised me a little at first because I do link hard English words to similar-sounding words in Spanish, but I realized I never really sit down to memorize things using structured tools like flashcards or rhymes.

This made me see that I learn well on the fly through context and people, but I need better tools to actually retain vocabulary long-term. My goal moving forward is to start using flashcards for new words, write them in sentences right after class, and check my notes every week to see if I actually remember them.
